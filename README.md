# 📊 Customer Purchasing Pattern Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior and demographic trends using Python.

The goal is to generate actionable business insights to improve decision-making in areas such as product strategy, customer segmentation, and shipping optimization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify top revenue-generating products  
- Analyze customer demographics (age, country)  
- Evaluate shipping cost efficiency  
- Compare income differences using statistical tests  
- Study correlation between income and purchasing behavior  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas  
- Matplotlib  
- SciPy  
- Google Colab  

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Revenue Analysis
Calculated total revenue and identified top-performing products.

### 🔹 Customer Segmentation
Analyzed customer distribution by age and country.

### 🔹 Shipping Optimization
Compared shipping costs based on delivery time.

### 🔹 Statistical Testing
- T-test (income difference by gender)  
- Chi-square test (product vs gender)

### 🔹 Correlation Analysis
Measured relationship between income and spending.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- A small number of products generate most of the revenue  
- Certain countries and age groups dominate the customer base  
- Shipping delays may increase operational costs  
- Income may influence purchasing behavior  

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing products  
- Target high-income customers  
- Improve shipping efficiency  
- Use data-driven marketing strategies
